Privacy Policy

VestEd — Last updated August 2026

VestEd ("the app") is a stock market education app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

VestEd does not have a backend server and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers. All data the app creates — your lesson progress, XP, streaks, achievements, and virtual portfolio (holdings and cash balance) — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties, because we don't collect any to begin with.
  • We don't require account creation to use the app.

Virtual trading

The stock portfolio in VestEd is entirely simulated for educational purposes. It uses virtual currency only — no real money, bank accounts, or brokerage connections are involved at any point.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled and shown locally by your device's operating system. No notification content is sent to or through our servers.

Data deletion

Since all data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes it completely. There is nothing stored elsewhere to delete.

Changes to this policy

If VestEd adds features that change how data is handled (for example, a future account system), this page will be updated before that change ships.
