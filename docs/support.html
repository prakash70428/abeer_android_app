Frequently asked questions

Is the money in the app real?
No. VestEd's portfolio and trading features use entirely virtual currency for practice and learning. No real money, bank accounts, or brokerage accounts are involved.

Do I need an account to use VestEd?
No account is required — your progress is saved directly on your device.

I found a bug or have a feature request.
Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead) and we'll look into it.

How do I reset my progress?
Uninstalling and reinstalling the app clears all locally stored progress, since nothing is stored on a server.
